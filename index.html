05 / NATIVE macOS APP

KeepAwake

Keep your Mac awake.

A native SwiftUI menu-bar utility for macOS 26.0 Tahoe or later on Intel and Apple silicon. It wraps /usr/bin/caffeinate for one-click indefinite awake or quick timers (30m, 1h, 2h, 4h, 8h, or custom), with live countdown/progress, global shortcuts, configurable display vs system idle sleep, launch at login, and no third-party background daemons.

Who is Ahmad Farid Abbas?

Ahmad Farid Abbas is an engineer, QA leader, and software builder. Since August 2020 he has been Quality Assurance Lead at Wise Systems, and he builds native macOS apps alongside his quality engineering work.

What apps has he built?

He has built five native macOS apps: KegPilot, a SwiftUI menu-bar app for Homebrew; Jotvella, a note-taking knowledge base with OneNote import; TaskZero, a menu-bar utility for closing running apps and verified background processes; Bauhaus Hands, a customizable clock screensaver; and KeepAwake, a menu-bar utility that keeps a Mac awake with timers.

What does he test, and with which tools?

At Wise Systems he tests SaaS applications, backend platforms, APIs, and integrations across web, mobile, and cloud systems, including AI/ML and LLM integrations. His tools include Postman, Pytest, JMeter, k6, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, Jira, TestRail, and Python.

What is the Senior QA Interview Master Guide?

It is an open-source interview preparation guide for Senior QA Engineers, SDETs, and QA Leads. It has 8 chapters and 200+ questions with model answers, frameworks, code examples, and diagrams, and is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. It is available on Ahmad's GitHub.

How does he use AI in his work?

He uses AI to support development, debugging, test creation and analysis, documentation, research, and problem-solving. Combined with Python, APIs, and automation, it helps turn repetitive work into simpler, more reliable systems.
